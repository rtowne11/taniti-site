About This Adventure

Discover one of Taniti's most dramatic natural landmarks on this guided volcano tour. Travel through lush tropical vegetation before reaching scenic overlooks with panoramic views of the island.

Your local guide will share information about Taniti's volcanic landscape, wildlife, and natural history along the way.
